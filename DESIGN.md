## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end artisanal floristry rooted in architectural warmth, classical grace, and modern editorial discretion. Tailored for discerning clients, interior tastemakers, and celebratory event curators in Oaxaca de Juárez, the experience evokes quiet luxury, tactile craftsmanship, and organic poise.

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Underpinnings**. Rather than sterile tech minimalism or loud floral e-commerce clichés, the interface behaves like an art book or an intimate atelier exhibition: expansive negative space, delicate typographic contrast, sun-warmed plaster tones, and tactile lacquer accents reminiscent of antique sealing wax. High-resolution floral arrangements and bespoke stems take prominence against luminous, unbleached backdrops.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the physical flagship boutique and bespoke signature brand artifacts:
- **Primary (`#6B1D2F` — Cire de Bourgogne):** A deep, lustrous wine taken from the brand's monogram wax seal. Used for primary CTAs, refined key moments, monogram badges, and high-impact focal accents.
- **Secondary (`#DF9B6D` — Cantera & Terracotta):** The sun-baked, warm peachy terracotta evocative of Oaxacan quarry stone and the boutique's street facade. Used for subtle highlights, interactive hovers, tags, and category navigation bars.
- **Tertiary (`#4A5D4E` — Feuillage Sage):** A muted botanical olive-eucalyptus providing a grounding organic presence for seasonal botanic indicators, live workshop chips, and secondary interaction indicators.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces (`#FDFBF7` canvas, `#F5EFEB` container, `#2B2220` ink):** A foundation of warm ivory alabaster paired with warm espresso-charcoal text instead of harsh pure black, ensuring gentle contrast and timeless warmth.

## Typography

The type system creates an editorial conversation between classical romanticism and effortless contemporary clarity:
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the legacy of Parisian floral salons with delicate serifs, generous apertures, and elegant high-contrast strokes. Reserved for titles, collection names, hero headlines, and pricing numbers.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and spacious sans-serif with subtle warm humanist touches. Ensures instant legibility in composition breakdowns, ordering steps, opening hours, and direct concierge chats.
- **Eyebrow & Monogram Labels (`label-caps`):** Formatted in clean uppercase with tracked-out spacing (`0.14em`) to mirror luxury house cataloguing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an art gallery catalog with a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and 4-column structure on mobile:
- **Atmospheric Margins:** Sections feature generous breathing room (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) between narrative modules, giving every arrangement photo the respect of an original artwork.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Staggered card layouts and alternating image-text pairing create cadence and prevent the appearance of standard retail e-commerce.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* 4 columns, 20px outer margin, sticky concierge pill for WhatsApp and Instagram orders.
  - *Tablet (768px – 1024px):* 8 columns, 32px outer margin, side-by-side floral catalog entries.
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* 12 columns, 64px outer margin, max container width of 1320px to prevent visual dilution on wide displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews artificial digital dropshadows in favor of natural light and tonal surfaces:
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is conveyed by transitioning from base ivory (`#FDFBF7`) to gentle limestone containers (`#F5EFEB`) and warm terracotta wash overlays.
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Cards and image containers rely on razor-thin, tactile borders (`1px solid rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.08)` or `1px solid rgba(223, 155, 109, 0.25)`) instead of heavy shadows.
- **Wax & Solitude Shadows:** Floating interactive anchors (such as floating booking pills and wax seal accents) use a highly diffused, sun-filtered amber shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(107, 29, 47, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The form language uses architectural subtlety. Sharp, tailored geometries are softened just enough (`0.25rem` / `rounded-soft`) to maintain warmth without falling into bubbly or overly casual design patterns:
- Small components, text inputs, chips, and editorial portrait frames adhere to `roundedness: 1` (`4px`).
- Circular exceptions are strictly preserved for the signature circular wax seal badge, flower stem swatch filters, and social avatar discs.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button (Wax Burgundy):** Background `#6B1D2F`, text `#FDFBF7`, 4px border radius. Padding `14px 28px`. Hover shifts smoothly to deep plum `#581825` with a subtle 1px border shift. Used for *"Pedir por WhatsApp"* and *"Reservar Bar Floral"*.
- **Secondary / Architectural Button:** Background transparent, text `#6B1D2F`, border `1px solid #DF9B6D`. Hover fills with soft peach tint `rgba(223, 155, 109, 0.12)`.
- **Text Link:** Serifs or uppercase sans-serif with a persistent 1px hairline underline offset by 4px in `#DF9B6D`.

### Floral & Variety Chips
- Subtle pill or soft rectangle (`4px` radius) with `rgba(74, 93, 78, 0.08)` background and `#4A5D4E` text.
- Active state uses filled forest-sage `#4A5D4E` with crisp ivory text.

### Product & Editorial Arrangement Cards
- Ivory surface (`#FFFFFF` or `#FDFBF7`) encased in a `1px` faint warm border (`#EFE6DC`).
- Generous photography ratio (4:5 or 3:4 portrait), zero image padding at top, followed by 20px interior spacing for title (`Playfair Display`), botanical composition notes, price, and instant direct-chat order button.

### Concierge Quick-Actions (WhatsApp & Instagram)
- Persistent floating dock on mobile: grounded in warm ivory frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), featuring a primary CTA to open a pre-filled WhatsApp concierge conversation alongside the Instagram link.

### Inputs & Booking Forms
- Clean underline-only or soft-boxed border in `rgba(43, 34, 32, 0.15)`. Active/focused input switches border to burgundy `#6B1D2F` without harsh blue focus rings.
- Placeholder text in muted botanical stone `#8C827A`.

### Wax Seal Monogram Badge
- Used as an authentic decorative stamp of origin for bespoke collections and opening announcements, rendered in rich burgundy with slight specular highlight and emboss-like framing.
